Lightning Lane
What it is and what it costs
Disney charges extra to skip the lines on popular rides. Every ride still has a free standby queue — Lightning Lane is the paid fast lane on top of that.
Multi Pass Most days
~$22–45 per person per day (varies by date)
Covers the majority of popular rides. You pre-book up to 3 rides before your visit and keep rolling bookings throughout the day.
Single Pass Key rides only
~$11–25 per person per ride
Covers a small number of the most popular rides that aren't included in Multi Pass. Buy up to two per day.
Premier Pass Skip it
Up to $449 per person at Magic Kingdom
Covers every Lightning Lane in the park with no scheduling. It can cost more than the park ticket itself. Multi Pass plus one well-chosen Single Pass gets you the same result for a fraction of the price.
Grace period you'll want to know
Your Lightning Lane return window stays valid for up to 119 minutes beyond the listed end time. If it says 3:00–4:00pm, you can tap in until 4:59pm. You have more flexibility than the app makes it look like.
