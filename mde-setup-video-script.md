# MDE Setup — Video Script
### The Park Edit / disneyparkedit.com
*8 individual clips, each living under its relevant Pre-Edit section*

---

## Clip 1 — Orientation: Three Lines Screen
*Screen: MDE three lines menu*

"This is My Disney Experience. Think of MDE as the tool Disney gives you. Your Park Edit is the strategy on top of it. My app opens here by default, but if yours lands on the map or home screen first, tap the three lines in the bottom right corner. Everything you need lives in here."

---

## Clip 2 — Create Profiles
*Screen: Three lines → scroll down → Account → My Family & Friends → Add*

"Before you do anything else, you're going to want to create profiles for your party including children under three. Even if kids under three don't need a park ticket, they do need a profile so restaurants can account for your full party. You can also do all of this at DisneyWorld.com if you prefer a bigger screen. To get there in the app, scroll down and tap Account, then My Family and Friends. There you will add everyone's name, age range, and avatar. Kids get really excited about these, so it's worth asking them who they want — but don't worry, you can change them later."

---

## Clip 3 — Link Tickets
*Screen: Three lines → Tickets and Passes tile (top left) → blue + → Link Tickets & Passes → existing linked tickets*

"Once your profiles are set up, link each person's ticket. You can also do this at DisneyWorld.com if you prefer a bigger screen. In the app, Tickets and Passes is the top left button on this screen. When you tap in, if nothing is showing up, tap the plus sign in the top right corner, then Link Tickets and Passes. You can scan the barcode on each ticket and assign it to the right person. If you don't have physical cards yet, you can type it in manually in the far right tab. Important: you cannot book any Lightning Lanes until every ticket is linked. If you bought through a third party, do this a few weeks out. Processing delays happen and you want to be ready."

---

## Clip 4 — Add a Payment Method
*Screen: Three lines → scroll down → Account → My Profile → Payment Method → blue +*

"You can also do this at DisneyWorld.com if you prefer a bigger screen. In the app, scroll down to the Account section and select My Profile, then Payment Method. Add a card here. This is how Disney charges Lightning Lane picks and mobile orders. You want to have this saved well before your Lightning Lane booking window opens, either 7 or 3 days before your trip. It's one less thing slowing you down on booking morning, when the best return windows for popular rides can go fast."

---

## Clip 5 — Link MagicBands (if using)
*Screen: Three lines → scroll down → Account → MagicBands & Cards → scroll to Learn More → Link Your MagicBand or Card*

"If you're using MagicBands, link them here — you can also do this at DisneyWorld.com. In the app, scroll down to Account and tap MagicBands & Cards. Then scroll down to the Learn More section and tap Link Your MagicBand or Card. Once linked, anyone can tap into rides, the hotel room, and Lightning Lanes without pulling out their phone. I find it easier to use my phone because I often already have it out, but kids do really like the MagicBand. It's not often they get to hold the keys."

---

## Clip 6 — Turn On Notifications
*Screen: Phone Settings → Disney World → Notifications → Allow Notifications*

"Go to your phone Settings and find Disney World — that's the My Disney Experience app. Turn notifications on. Without this, return window reminders and dining alerts don't reach you. You end up checking the app constantly instead of just showing up when it's time."

---

## Clip 7 — Turn On Location
*Screen: Phone Settings → Disney World → Location → While Using the App*

"Back in your phone Settings — find Disney World again and set location to While Using the App. Two things stop working without it: mobile ordering and the Lightning Lane tap stiles. Both matter on a park day."

---

## Clip 8 — Orientation + Mobile Ordering
*Screen: Three lines → My Plans; three lines → Order Food tile → restaurant → Schedule for Later → pickup window → items → I'm Here*

"Last thing — find these before you arrive. Three lines, then My Plans. That's where your Lightning Lane bookings live.

For mobile ordering, you're going to stay right here on this screen. See the button that looks like your phone is setting the table? Tap that. It will default to showing restaurants near your location, but use the filters to find what's nearby if you need to. Tap your restaurant, then tap Schedule for Later first and choose your pickup window before you start selecting items — otherwise it defaults to as soon as possible. Once you've picked your window, choose your items. When you're close, tap I'm Here and your food will be ready. You never have to stand in a queue. Your Park Edit will tell you exactly when to order — you just need to know where that button lives."
